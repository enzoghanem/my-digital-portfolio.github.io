SkinSpirit may want to attract more customers through search and digital campaigns, but reaching local audiences consistently can be challenging. A Digital Marketing Coordinator could help improve visibility, coordinate campaigns, and turn online interest into bookings.

03 / Proposed project

“Find Your Glow” — a 4-week campaign concept

A hypothetical campaign designed to introduce local audiences to SkinSpirit's services and encourage them to explore relevant treatment information and booking options.

Discover

Review the customer journey, local search terms, website calls to action, and service landing pages.

Create

Develop helpful educational content, local social posts, and consistent campaign messages.

Measure

Track traffic, engagement, and booking clicks; improve messaging based on performance.

Content calendar

Week 1Audience research, keyword ideas, and campaign planning.
Week 2Educational posts and optimized service-page recommendations.
